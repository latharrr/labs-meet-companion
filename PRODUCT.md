# Lathar Labs meeting companion

For Deepanshu to inspect a Google Meet record, understand decisions and review what the bot may say or send. The current local build is operator-controlled, with no automatic external effects. The sample is invented dialogue, not a live meeting. Primary task: read notes with source evidence, inspect the transcript and review queued messages. Do not market unfinished functionality as ready. Technical integration choices: self-hosted Vexa, Groq speech and AI, Resend email, separately configured OpenWA bridge.
