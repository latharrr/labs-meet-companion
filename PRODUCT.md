# Lathar Labs meeting companion

A review-first meeting companion with a customer-workspace foundation: source-linked transcript review, notes and exact follow-up approvals. The synthetic demo sends nothing. Customer isolation, Google login and an owner-only OTP test path are implemented in source; the hosted meeting engine is not running and public launch is not accepted. See README.md and docs/STATUS.md for current limits. No unattended joining, messaging or delivery is implied.
