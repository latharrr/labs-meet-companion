03 / YOUR CONTROL

Your meeting.
Your record.
Your call.

Good meeting software should make the next step clearer, not take control away from you.

01

Consent comes first.

A visible meeting bot, organizer authority and required participant notice. No quiet recording or lobby bypass.

02

Evidence, not invention.

Keep the transcript close. Check a source before accepting a summary, decision or action candidate.

03

Review before it leaves.

Approve the words and destination. A send request isn't proof of delivery, and an uncertain send isn't retried blindly.
